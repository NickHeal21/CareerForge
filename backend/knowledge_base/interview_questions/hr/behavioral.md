# HR & Behavioral Interview Questions

## Common HR Questions
- Tell me about yourself (structured: Present → Past → Future)
- Why do you want to work here?
- What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
- Why should we hire you?
- What is your expected salary?
- Describe a challenging situation and how you handled it
- How do you handle pressure and deadlines?
- What motivates you?
- Do you have any questions for us?

## Behavioral (STAR Method)
Use the STAR framework: Situation, Task, Action, Result

- Tell me about a time you showed leadership
- Describe a conflict with a teammate and how you resolved it
- Give an example of a project you're most proud of
- Tell me about a time you failed and what you learned
- Describe a situation where you had to learn something quickly
- Tell me about a time you went above and beyond
- How have you handled a disagreement with a manager?

## Situational Questions
- If you disagree with your manager's technical decision, what would you do?
- A critical bug is found 1 hour before release. How do you handle it?
- You're assigned two high-priority tasks with the same deadline. What do you do?
- A teammate isn't pulling their weight. How do you address it?

## Tips for HR Interviews
1. Research the company thoroughly
2. Prepare 3-5 STAR stories that cover multiple scenarios
3. Be specific — use numbers and outcomes
4. Show enthusiasm without being generic
5. Always have 2-3 thoughtful questions to ask
6. Be honest about gaps — frame them as growth opportunities
